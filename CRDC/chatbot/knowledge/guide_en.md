## [about] About the guide and the center
<!-- service: general | source: Procedural guide – Introduction | kw: what does the center do, about, overview, available services, list of services -->
The procedural guide explains the procedures for the services provided by the Consulting, Research and Development Center (CRDC) at the Sadat Academy for Management Sciences, to ensure transparency and make it easier for researchers to deal with the center from the moment they request a service until they receive the final certificates.

The center offers five services:
1. Refereeing and publishing research papers.
2. Language proofreading of theses.
3. Statistical analysis and statistical review of theses.
4. Digital Transformation Fundamentals certificate.
5. Similarity (plagiarism) measurement.

## [thesis_vs_paper] Thesis vs. research paper
<!-- service: general | source: Procedural guide – Thesis vs. short research paper | kw: difference between thesis and paper, article, master thesis, phd dissertation, number of pages -->
A full thesis (Master's or PhD) differs from a research paper (article) in form, size and content:
• A thesis is more comprehensive and longer, including methodology chapters and appendices: roughly 100 to 150 pages.
• A research paper briefly presents the method and results: roughly 20 to 30 pages, intended for publication in refereed journals.

## [general_procedures] General procedures for all services
<!-- service: general | source: Procedural guide – General procedures for all services | kw: how do I start, how to apply, steps, open a file, payment permit, receipt, pay, treasury, bank, procedure -->
General procedures for all services:
1. A file is opened in the researcher's name when they apply for their first service at the center.
2. The researcher fills in the data form and signs the pledge to follow the center's rules.
3. The researcher gets a payment permit from the center (main headquarters in Maadi).
4. The researcher pays the fees: Egyptian researchers at the Academy treasury in EGP, international researchers at the approved bank in US dollars.
5. After paying, the researcher hands the original receipt to the center so the requested service can start.
6. When all required reviews are done and the researcher has passed the Digital Transformation Fundamentals exam, all required certificates are delivered on paper, officially approved and stamped by the center.

## [degree_requirements] Services required for each degree
<!-- service: general | source: Procedural guide – Certificates required for each degree | kw: what do I need, requirements for my degree, academic masters, academic phd, professional masters, professional doctorate, how many papers to publish -->
Certificates each researcher must obtain to fulfil the degree requirements:
• Academic PhD: language review, statistical review, statistical analysis, Digital Transformation certificate, similarity measurement, and publishing two research papers.
• Academic Master's: language review, statistical review, statistical analysis, Digital Transformation certificate, similarity measurement, and publishing one research paper.
• Professional Doctorate: the Digital Transformation certificate only.
• Professional Master's: the Digital Transformation certificate only.

## [pub_journals] Refereeing and publishing: the journals
<!-- service: publication | source: Procedural guide – 1. Refereeing and publishing research | kw: publishing, refereeing, journals, journal link, journal rating, 7/7, accredited, where to publish -->
The center offers refereeing and publishing of research papers in:
• The Administrative Research Journal: https://jso.journals.ekb.eg
• The Sadat Journal for Administrative and Financial Research: https://sjsaf.journals.ekb.eg

Both journals are accredited by the Supreme Council of Universities with a 7/7 rating, the highest classification for scientific journals. Researchers can visit both journal websites to read already-published papers as a guide to the scientific form and correct methodology of a publishable paper.

## [pub_steps] Steps for refereeing and publishing
<!-- service: publication | source: Procedural guide – 1. Refereeing and publishing procedures | kw: how to publish, publishing steps, upload paper, create account on journal, referee result, revisions, acceptance certificate -->
Steps for refereeing and publishing:
1. Go to the center's office (main headquarters in Maadi).
2. Fill in the "Research Refereeing Request" form at the center.
3. Get a payment permit from the center and pay the fees (Egyptians at the treasury, international researchers at the approved bank), then return the receipt to the center to start the service.
4. After paying, go to the website of one of the two journals (https://jso.journals.ekb.eg or https://sjsaf.journals.ekb.eg), create your account, and upload the paper ready for refereeing.
5. After electronic refereeing by accredited referees, you are notified of the result by email.
6. If revisions are requested, make them and resubmit the paper.
7. If accepted, the center issues an approved, stamped certificate confirming acceptance for publication.

## [pub_rules] Refereeing and publishing requirements
<!-- service: publication | source: Procedural guide – Rules and requirements for refereeing and publishing | kw: publishing rules, conditions, publish in english, language of paper, paper already published elsewhere, copyright, republish, declaration -->
Rules and requirements for refereeing and publishing:
• Papers must be relevant to the aims of the center's two journals.
• Papers are accepted in Arabic or English, provided they have not been published or submitted for publication elsewhere; the researcher signs a declaration to that effect.
• The paper must be prepared in Microsoft Word following the specified formatting.
• The journal keeps all publishing rights; the material may not be republished without written permission from the journal's administration.

## [pub_pay_once] Why are refereeing and publishing fees paid at once?
<!-- service: publication | source: Center clarifications | kw: pay refereeing and publishing together, at once, pay before acceptance, what if rejected -->
The refereeing and publishing fees are paid together at once as a facilitation for researchers, although normally the researcher would pay the refereeing fee first and wait for the paper to be accepted before paying the publishing fee.

## [formatting] Paper formatting specifications
<!-- service: publication | source: Procedural guide – Paper preparation specifications | kw: formatting, margins, font, font size, simplified arabic, times new roman, page numbers, template -->
Paper specifications (Microsoft Word):
• Top and bottom margins: 2.54 cm (Arabic and English).
• Right and left margins: 3.17 cm (Arabic and English).
• Font: Simplified Arabic for Arabic papers, Times New Roman for English papers.
• Main title size: 18 bold (Arabic), 16 bold (English).
• Subtitle size: 14 (Arabic), 12 (English).
• Body text size: 14 (Arabic), 12 (English).
• Page numbers: 13 (Arabic and English).

## [ling_steps] Steps for language proofreading of theses
<!-- service: proofreading | source: Procedural guide – 2. Language proofreading | kw: proofreading, language review, editing, where to send thesis, email, proofreading steps -->
Steps for language proofreading of theses:
1. Go to the center (main headquarters in Maadi) to get a payment permit for the service.
2. Pay the fees (Egyptians at the treasury, international researchers at the approved bank in US dollars), then return to the center and hand over the original receipt.

## [dig_about] Digital Transformation Fundamentals certificate: what is it?
<!-- service: digital | source: Procedural guide – 4. Digital Transformation Fundamentals certificate | kw: digital transformation, digital certificate, course, exam, degree requirement, supreme council of universities, online course -->
The Digital Transformation Fundamentals certificate is accredited by the Supreme Council of Universities and is a requirement for being awarded any degree from Egyptian universities.

The service has two options:
1. Take the online training course for one month, then sit the exams.
2. Sit the exams directly without the course.

## [cit_threshold] Acceptable similarity percentage and what if I exceed it
<!-- service: similarity | source: Procedural guide – 5 + Center clarifications | kw: acceptable percentage, what percent, 25%, similarity too high, re-check, similarity report, reduce percentage -->
The scientifically acceptable similarity percentage is below 25%.
If the percentage exceeds the acceptable limit, the full similarity report is sent to the researcher to revise the work and reduce the percentage. After revising, the researcher may request the service again and pay the fees again.

## [pledge] The researcher's pledge
<!-- service: general | source: Procedural guide – Researcher's pledge | kw: pledge, declaration, what do I commit to, confidentiality, draft versions, final versions, extra pages difference -->
The researcher signs a pledge to fully comply with the following:
1. Deliver the final versions of theses to the Postgraduate Studies Administration after linguistic and statistical review, and not submit draft versions of theses or papers before the review and proofreading procedures through the center are complete.
2. Declare that the paper to be published in the center's journals has not been published or submitted for publication elsewhere.
3. Fully comply with the instructions of the Digital Transformation Fundamentals exam committees and keep order during the committees.
4. Pay any financial difference if the paper exceeds 25 pages or the thesis exceeds 150 pages, before receiving the final certificates.
5. Keep the procedures and data confidential, and not circulate any copies or reports issued by the center except with official permission.

The researcher also declares that all information provided is correct and accepts full responsibility for violating any of these terms.
